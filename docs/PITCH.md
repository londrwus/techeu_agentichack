# Orbit: 2-minute pitch

Tone: calm, confident, never pushy. About 290 spoken words, which is about 2 minutes. **Bold** = say it with weight. [CLICK] = action.

**Before going on stage:**
- Open `/landing`, `#/news` and `#/mission` once. This caches the globe and warms up the Modal containers.
- Keep the tabs in the order used below.

---

[Landing page on screen, globe turning]

> Orbit sees it first. It's **satellite intelligence for commodities, fully agentic**. **Google Gemini** sees, **Jev** judges, **Modal** scales."

### 0:15–0:30 · Overview (one screen, whole market)
[CLICK: Open Orbit → Overview]

> "Twenty regions watched from orbit and 1,840 satellite images, turned into one view.
> For a **business**, this is supply risk before it lands on your P&L. For **you**, it's your weekly basket: **up about 3 % in six months.**"

### 0:30–0:55 · GPU & Gadgets (the hook for this room)
[CLICK: GPU & Gadgets. Drag the Stargate before/after slider.]

> "Let's talk about something everyone here buys: **GPUs**.
> That's the Stargate site in Abilene, 2019 versus today. Seen from space, that's where your graphics card is going.

### 0:55–1:10 · Rent Radar (it's personal)
[CLICK: Rent Radar. Zoom into a neighbourhood and click Peckham.]

> "Same engine, on the most painful price in London: **rent**. New construction measured from orbit, hexagon by hexagon.
> **Peckham: £2,475 today, about £2,610 next year.** A landlord sees pressure; a tenant sees when to lock in a contract."

### 1:10–1:30 · News hub (Jev's speed moment)
[CLICK: News hub → **Scan the news**. Let it run.]

> "Satellites show the ground. Markets also react to **words**.
> Watch: **four thousand fresh headlines**, and Jev judges every one of them for relevance, commodity, price direction and severity, with calibrated probabilities.
> **Sixteen thousand judgments in about six seconds**, on fifty Modal containers, for **less than eight cents**."

### 1:30–1:48 · Mission Control (the agentic core)
[CLICK: Mission Control → **Scan now**. Talk while the counters race.]

> "And this is the agent loop live. Modal fans out to **up to 100 containers**, pulls fresh Sentinel-2 imagery for every region, and Jev judges the news. **Google TimesFM on GPUs** forecasts, and Gemini explains.
> No analyst team and no waiting for the monthly report."

### 1:48–2:00 · Close
[Optional: flash Track record]

> **Orbit: know tomorrow's prices today.**"

## Backup lines (if a judge asks)
- **"How accurate is it?"** On unseen 2023–26 data it gets direction right 65 % of the time and gives an honest range. It flags about 7 in 10 big price rises (above 15 %), and we publish every method we tried on the Track record page, including the ones that lost.
- **"What's Jev doing that an LLM can't?"** Typed, calibrated judgments at about 2,500 per second for fractions of a cent. Code decides using real probabilities, not generated text.
- **"Why Modal?"** Sentinel-2 processing on up to 100 CPU containers, TimesFM on L4 GPUs, a daily scan, and the whole app is served from Modal.
- **"Where is it real, and where is it synthetic?"** Satellite, news, commodity prices and Jev are real. The GPU street-price series is curated from public sources. Rents are modelled. Everything is in `docs/MODEL.md`.
